Engineering Trust & Assurance

Roughly four in five generative-AI pilots fail to reach production value — and the cause is almost never the model. It's missing data foundations, absent leadership commitment, and tools bolted onto old workflows. What follows pairs named engineering disciplines with the architected governance mechanisms already built into HSGA 3.0 and the PLM console — together they are what make autonomous automation safe to delegate and attorney judgment trustworthy to rely on.

The same lifecycle-console logic that gates engineering maturity now gates legal AI initiatives: Discover → Define → Design → Develop → Deploy → Run, scored against target maturity per discipline. Risk metrology, applied to malpractice & compliance exposure

Likelihood × impact, from prompt to precedent

Every open gap between an initiative's target and assessed maturity is scored as a risk, the same way the PLM console scores engineering risk: likelihood (L) from how far off target and whether it's even been assessed, impact (I) from criticality, release-facing phase and discipline, exposure E = L × I, banded low / moderate / high / critical. The escalation ladder and gauge below are computed live from every initiative's actual gaps — not fixed numbers.
