Community Immunity Project — start here

Your community has an immune system.
This tool helps you activate it.

When a data center moves into a town without warning — draining water, spiking electricity bills, polluting air — the community's first response looks exactly like what happens inside your body when a virus arrives. This dashboard uses that parallel to help community leaders understand what's happening, what phase they're in, and what to do next.
The core idea — your body already knows how to fight this

When a virus enters your body, it doesn't announce itself. It moves quietly, extracts what it needs, and relies on your cells not recognizing the threat until it's too late to stop it easily. Sound familiar?

AI data centers arrive the same way. A chamber of commerce announcement — no vote, no environmental study, no community input. By the time residents notice the water pressure dropping and the electric bills doubling, the corporation has signed contracts, received tax breaks, and has lawyers on retainer.

The Community Immunity Project applies what biology already knows: a body that has fought a virus once — and recorded how it did it — fights the next one faster, harder, and with far less damage. This tool is the recording system.

What the simulation actually does
The 18-month simulation is the heart of this tool. It takes four real factors — how fast your community organizes, how large the corporation's legal budget is, whether you have access to the Registry of past cases, and how much legal support you have — and projects what happens over the next 18 months.
The four inputs you control
Organization speed — how quickly your community can hold meetings, coordinate, and act together. A trained coalition moves at speed 8. A neighborhood that just started talking moves at 3.
Corporate budget — how much money the corporation has to fight back. xAI: 9. A regional data center startup: 4.
Registry access — do you have the Memphis playbook? Do you know which laws apply, which tests to run, which lawyers to call? This is the single highest-leverage variable.
Legal aid depth — do you have a nonprofit law firm partner, or are you on your own? This determines whether you can take the fight all the way to a documented outcome.
The most important thing the simulation shows
With Registry access at 0, a community spends most of the first six months rediscovering what other communities already learned. They find out about thermal imaging in month 4 instead of week 1. They send the legal notice in month 6 instead of month 2.
That four-month delay is the difference between getting concessions and signing an NDA. The simulation makes that gap visible as a chart, a number, and a clear story you can tell a funder or a legislator.
Try this: go to the 18-month simulation, leave everything else the same, and drag Registry access from 0 to 7. Watch the outcome change from "Partial loss" to "Partial win." That's what the Public Harm Registry does.
Plain-language glossary — terms used throughout this tool
EHI scoreAntigen signature
Exploitation Harm Index. A 0–100 number that measures how much damage a data center is causing across five dimensions: air, water, grid, noise, and regulatory capture. Memphis scores 74. Criminal liability under S.E.P.A. starts at 76.
Public Harm RegistryImmunological memory
A permanent public database of documented cases — what the corporation did, what harm it caused, what legal actions worked. When your community accesses it, you don't have to start from scratch. You start from where Memphis left off.
S.E.P.A.The immune system's rulebook
The Systemic Exploitation Prevention Act — proposed legislation that establishes strict liability for exploitation, creates the Office of Harm Measurement, and imposes criminal penalties on executives whose decisions produce irreversible harm.
60-Day NoticeFirst adaptive response
A Notice of Intent to Sue under the Clean Air Act or Clean Water Act. It's free to file, gives the corporation 60 days to negotiate, and often forces concessions within 20 days. Memphis: filed June 17, 2025. Concessions followed within six weeks.
Remediation bondAntitoxin held in reserve
Money the corporation deposits before construction — enough to clean up the site if they abandon it or go bankrupt. You demand this before the permit is signed, not after the damage is done.
Qui tamImmune cell reward system
A legal provision that lets any citizen bring a lawsuit on behalf of the government and receive 25–40% of what's recovered. Under S.E.P.A., a successful qui tam action against a data center corporation means the community that documented the harm gets paid from the corporation's disgorgement.
OHMThe body's diagnostic lab
Office of Harm Measurement. The independent agency under S.E.P.A. that calculates EHI scores, accepts public complaints, and refers findings to the Office of Exploitation Prosecution. Funded entirely from enforcement collections — it can't be defunded by a captured legislature.
