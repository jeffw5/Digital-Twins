Marketing Ontology Lineage

The holarchy behind ontology-domain-marketing, read left to right from parent to child. BFO 2020 and the OBO Relations Core sit above the unifying ontology, ontology-core-domain, which declares the roots of every core ontology (ontology-core-*) and core taxonomy (taxonomy-core-*); vocabulary-core-domain grounds it. The marketing domain inherits all of it: vocabulary-domain-marketing hangs off vocabulary-core-domain, taxonomy-domain-marketing and taxonomy-domain-marketing-ci specialise the core taxonomy and ontology classes, and ontology-domain-marketing imports them all. The ontology was built first, as a catalog of 23 marketing services with their enabling processes; the terms those processes need became the domain taxonomies and vocabularies. Competitive intelligence services, processes and terms were surfaced from the PEO Competitive Graph. New relations are declared in BFO Relations first.
