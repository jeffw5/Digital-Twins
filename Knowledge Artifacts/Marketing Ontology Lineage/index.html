Marketing Ontology Lineage

How the marketing domain stands on the Domain Reference Model, BFO 2020 and the OBO Relations Core. The ontology was built first, as a catalog of 23 marketing services, each enabled by its supporting processes and enabling a matching capability. The terms each process creates, uses, changes, targets or promotes then became the classes of two marketing taxonomies, grounded in two marketing vocabularies: one for general marketing and one for competitive intelligence, whose services, processes and terms were surfaced from the PEO Competitive Graph. New relations are declared in BFO Relations first and then referenced. Edges come from the files: owl:imports, dc:subject grounding, subclass anchors, relation use in SHACL shapes and SKOS mappings.
