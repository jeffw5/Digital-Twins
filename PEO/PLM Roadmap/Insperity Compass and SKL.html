Executive vision

Compass is Insperity's AI. The Semantic Knowledge Layer is its true north.

The AI Calibration Engine

The Semantic Knowledge Layer: Our Enterprise True North

Eliminating AI drift with a Semantic Knowledge Layer (SKL)

Just as a physical compass relies on Earth's magnetic field to prevent cumulative drift, an enterprise AI requires an external, unchanging frame of reference to stay on course. Without it, Large Language Models naturally drift toward hallucinations, outdated context, and conflicting departmental definitions. Our Semantic Knowledge Layer acts as the magnetic field for our AI—anchoring every query to governed business logic, verified data relationships, and real-time enterprise truth so leadership can trust every output.

Drift: hallucinations

Anchored to governed business logic

Every Compass answer is grounded on rules in the SKL and scored by MTBH, which measures drift directly. Below threshold, the circuit breaker hands the case to a person.

Drift: outdated context

Anchored to current enterprise truth

Every rule and asset carries effective dates and a version. A law change reaches compiled policy in 48 hours or less by Phase 3, and expired versions are never cited.

Drift: conflicting definitions

Anchored to one vocabulary

One canonical definition per term across Workday, payroll, Microsoft 365 and Visier. Insperity and customer alternate labels are kept separately, so the core stays lean.

Stack independence and interoperability

The SKL owns contracts, not products. Canonical knowledge lives in W3C and open standards, and every vendor surface receives a generated projection that carries the source URI. Compass can run on Copilot today, Workday stays the system of record for HRScale clients, and Insperity can change model or cloud providers later without rewriting the knowledge.

ADR-01

Canonical truth lives in open standards

OWL, SKOS, SHACL and LKIF are the source. Fabric IQ ontologies, Workday business-process conditions, Visier definitions, Purview glossary terms and dbt tests are generated from it.

Consequence: nobody hand-edits a projection. The mediation holon detects drift and flags it in CI.

ADR-02

Calibration sits in the request path, outside any model vendor

Every Compass agent, whatever product it is built in, reaches data and actions through the SKL calibration gateway.

Consequence: moving Compass to another model or copilot product changes nothing about what is allowed.

ADR-03

Every agent is an identity with a governed profile

Agents get a directory identity and a profile listing allowed tools, tenants and autonomy level, just as employees get personal models.

Consequence: agent sprawl becomes visible and auditable. Unregistered agents cannot reach SKL services.

ADR-04

Replaceability is tested, not claimed

Any adapter can be replaced within one quarter without changing L1 or L2 holons. One swap is exercised at every phase gate.

Consequence: lock-in risk is measured and reported to the ARB each phase.

ADR-05

The graph is reached only through SKL services

No external service, third party, system or individual queries the graph store directly. Applications use the semantic services, and operations staff maintain vocabularies, taxonomies, ontologies and mappings through provisioned screens that log, audit and enforce semantic governance.

Consequence: every read and every change is attributable, and the store can be replaced behind the services without breaking consumers.

Decisions needed and risks

Decisions that set Phase 0 scope

  • How Compass calls the SKL. Which Compass agents and Copilot Studio flows route through the calibration gateway first.
  • Microsoft licensing in place. Whether Purview, Fabric capacity and Copilot Studio are already licensed determines which adapters come first.
  • System of record by client segment. HRScale clients on Workday and others on Insperity's platform sets the adapter order and the parity scope.
  • Council membership. Legal, compliance, domain owners and IT architecture, with the authority to approve rule changes.

Top risks and mitigations

  • Benefits answers are high stakes. MTBH thresholds, circuit-breaker escalation to people, and Simplex holds on anything irreversible.
  • Compass agents bypass the SKL. The agent registry and gateway path are a Phase 1 gate; unregistered agents cannot reach SKL services.
  • State AI and hiring laws keep changing. The regulatory change discovery holon and AEDT audits treat them as rules like any other.
  • Semantics become a bottleneck. ORSD templates, governed editing screens, and a council with a published cycle-time target.
