Appreciative Inquiry for VOC

Two ways in, one output. Interviewers use the guided capture to run a 4-D conversation and code what they hear. Interviews already conducted elsewhere come through the Semantic Services inbound pipeline, which segments, redacts, classifies, extracts and resolves them against the same model, then hands them to a steward before anything is committed to the graph. Both land as the same interview, excerpt and evidence records.
