Dynamic review routing

Items are routed by policy, persona, modality and confidence. Content holds go to Privacy, Compliance or Legal. Label candidates go to the capturing persona's vocabulary steward. Stewards can approve, remap to a different core concept, or reject. Every decision is logged as a change record.

Persona vocabulary extension graphs

Design: alternate labels kept separate from the core

The core vocabulary ( concepts in this demo) is read-only to inbound services. Each persona's terms and phrases are kept as skosxl:Label resources in that persona's own named graph, g:ext/<persona>. Each label points to its core concept and carries status, confidence, provenance and usage.

This keeps core and domain ontologies from becoming overloaded with labels, which protects performance and keeps complexity down. It also lets each persona's extension be piloted, governed and scaled on its own.
