Transformation thesis

From Siloed Pilots to AI Embedded in the Core

The Hartford is executing a structured, multi-year digital transformation — moving data, analytics and generative AI out of isolated side projects and directly into underwriting risk assessment, claims adjudication and policy administration. A multi-year AWS cloud migration and a unified data foundation carry the weight; governance and human-in-the-loop design keep it trustworthy at scale.

      Stack at a glance

      Semantic layer buildout top-down meets bottom-up

      A pragmatic, incremental build — not a big-bang ontology project. Thin, use-case-driven slices ship end to end (business term → ontology → graph → API → persona value) starting with the domains carrying the most disparate terminology, while the core vocabulary stays small enough to actually govern.

      Top-down · unified business knowledge

        Bottom-up · unified data semantic layer

          Where they meet → Both tracks converge on one knowledge graph — business meaning validated from the top, source data verified from the bottom — so neither the ontology nor the graph drifts from the other.

          Extensible domains starting with three thin slices

          Each domain extends a small, shared core vocabulary (Policy, Coverage, Claim, Party, Customer) rather than modeling from scratch — proof the pattern scales is a later domain shipping faster than the first. Tap a card for scope, core classes and the competency question it proves.

          Harmonizing disparate business terms one canonical meaning per concept

          The same underlying concept goes by different names across Commercial, Personal, Specialty and Benefits — the ontology gives each one a single canonical definition and a formal relationship to its neighbors, instead of leaving five systems to define "coverage" five different ways.

          Semantic governance the safeguard for consistent understanding

          Rules, constraints and validation live in the graph itself — SHACL shapes, SWRL rules and OWL restrictions — so governance is enforced the moment data enters or is queried, not as a separate review process. This is what lets a human underwriter and an AI model trust the same graph: the same rules that keep the data internally consistent are the rules that keep an AI's inference from silently going wrong.

          Trustworthy inferencing, by construction
          Because SHACL shapes double as the access-control boundary (PII-tagged properties are only visible to authorized shapes), governance and access control aren't a bolt-on layer — they're properties of the graph itself, applied identically whether the query comes from an underwriter's dashboard or a GenAI retrieval call.

          Built for downstream teams query the graph without being a semantic expert

          The graph's expressiveness stays available to the teams who need it directly; everyone else gets a normal developer experience.

          Operational efficiency manual data assembly, before & after

          The value shows up first and most concretely in the two personas who spend the most time assembling context by hand today.

          Governance & responsible AI

          AI handles the routine; humans own the complex

          Model risk management, explainability, fairness and PII protection operate as one governance plane — every AI-assisted decision in underwriting and claims carries lineage back to a documented, risk-tiered model. The matrix maps machine-led vs. human-led decisions across routine and complex cases. Tap a cell for its governance analytics.

          Decision ownership matrix — tap a cell

          Phased AI autonomy earned, not granted

          AI does not arrive autonomous. It climbs a gated ladder — each phase widens the circuit-breaker envelope only after the system proves itself on the five assurance dimensions (WHO · WHAT · WHEN · WHERE · WHY) and clears a reliability gate. Promotion is a governance decision, not a configuration flag — every model is also risk-tiered under model risk management before it earns its first phase.

          Five-dimensional circuit breaker · assurance level by phase

          Business lines · adoption at their own pace

          The model is shared — the pace of adoption isn't

          A shared cloud-and-AI foundation federates across every business line, while each line adopts embedded AI at the pace its risk profile and data maturity allow. Personal Lines leads on volume; Specialty leads on human judgment; every line inherits the same governance on day one.
