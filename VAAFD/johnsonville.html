Johnsonville Medical High School

A science campus where children who had nowhere to go can learn to heal their nation.

Photo placeholder
Johnsonville campus, foundation and walls

Why this school

Liberia had the highest death rate in West Africa during the Ebola outbreak, in large part because there were too few trained health workers. A school that teaches biology, chemistry, physics and first aid gives young people skills that protect their whole community.
