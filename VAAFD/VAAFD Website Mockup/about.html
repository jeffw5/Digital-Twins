Our story

Built by refugees. Led by Liberians. Open to the world.

VAAFD began with one teacher and one idea: no child should be turned away from learning because of war, poverty or loss.

Mission

To bring free, high-quality education to Liberia's most vulnerable children and youth, and to form long-term partnerships with communities, donors and volunteers that produce sustainable social, environmental and economic initiatives.

Vision

Liberian leaders who are educated, self-reliant and proud of their culture, driving the nation's recovery from within.

Principles

How we work with partners

Mission sovereignty

Our vision and curriculum stay at the center. Funding does not dictate educational philosophy.

Reciprocal learning

Partners learn from our community engagement and cultural pedagogy as much as we learn from them.

Capacity building

Every partnership strengthens Liberian expertise. We avoid creating permanent dependency.

Transparency

Communities understand and approve partnership terms. Our reports are public.

Diversified funding

No single funder is allowed to exceed 20% of our budget.
