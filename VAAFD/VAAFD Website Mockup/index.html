Our journey

From a refugee camp classroom to a school with room to grow

  1. 2003–05

    A school begins

    Karrus Hayes, a refugee in Ghana, builds a tuition-free school for children no one else was teaching. VAAFD is founded in 2005.

  2. Return

    Home to Liberia

    Families repatriate and the school follows them, running K–12 in Paynesville, Monrovia for children who had no path back to the classroom.

  3. Today

    Scholars and a new campus

    Scholarship students reach university at Cuttington, and a second, larger campus in Johnsonville is under construction.

  4. 2026

    Built to last

    Now a U.S. 501(c)(3), VAAFD is adding transparent reporting, a development team, and partnerships to sustain every student.
