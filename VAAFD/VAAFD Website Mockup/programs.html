Programs

One mission, four ways to reach a child

From the first day of school to a university diploma, each program closes a gap that would otherwise end a child's education.

Carolyn A. Miller Schools

A complete, tuition-free K–12 program in Paynesville, Monrovia, serving about 315 students confirm. The school is one of few in Liberia that educates children who cannot pay.

  • Qualified teachers, honored and supported
  • Textbooks, uniforms and learning materials
  • Preparation for national exams
  • African-centered curriculum in development

University scholarships

Graduates who would otherwise stop at high school continue their studies at Cuttington University in Bong County. VAAFD scholars report on their progress each semester, and several are approaching graduation.

  • Tuition and basic costs for each scholar
  • Mentoring and check-ins with VAAFD leadership
  • Scholar stories shared with donors (with consent)

Orphan and Ebola-orphan care

Children who lost parents to civil war, disease or Ebola need more than a seat in class. VAAFD supports their schooling and connects them with caring adults, including a program for orphans in Bong County.

  • School fees, supplies and meals
  • Safe, supervised learning environments

Johnsonville Medical High School

A science-focused campus on a full acre outside Monrovia. Students will study biology, chemistry and physics and graduate with first-aid skills and a role in preventing the next outbreak.

  • Foundation and block-work under way
  • Future lab, library and clinic-skills rooms
  • Scale-up path from the Monrovia model

2026 Living Lab pilots

Three campus pilots that connect learning to health, energy and food. Status: planning. Location and partners to be confirmed before publishing.

Planning

Envision Africa

Community vision screening, a Vision Passport health record for every student, and a referral path to surgical care.

Planned measure: students and residents screened per year

Planning

Living Lab Energy

A solar microgrid to power the school and give the community paid phone charging, with revenue reinvested in the school.

Planned measure: kWh generated, study hours after sunset

Planning

Regenerative Farming

A chemical-free food forest and water-reuse system that feeds students and funds an endowment.

Planned measure: zero-chemical meals served
