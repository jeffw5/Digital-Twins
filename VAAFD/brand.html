Brand kit

A new look that says: awake, rooted, trustworthy

Three original logo concepts, a palette drawn from sunrise, forest and earth, and a type pairing that feels both warm and credible. Choose one and the whole mockup updates.

Logo concepts

Choose a mark

Select "Use this logo" to preview it in the header and footer of every page. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

A · Dawn Eye

An open eye with a sun for an iris and rays above the lid: "vision awake." Strongest link to the name.

B · Rising Page

A sun rising from an open book: education as the dawn. Most direct for a school.

C · Awake A

A monogram "A" with a sun for a crossbar and a leaf at the peak: growth and a sustainable future. Works well as an app icon.

All three are original sketches for discussion. Have a designer refine and test the chosen mark in one color, on dark backgrounds, and at favicon size.

Principles

How the brand behaves

Dignity first

Children are students and leaders, never props. Always use consent, first names and respectful framing.

Evidence over adjectives

Every claim carries a number, a date or a document. Targets are never presented as results.

Rooted and global

Liberian voices lead the story; international partners are welcomed as collaborators.
