    Governance & traceability

    Four-fold governance over every decision

    Data, semantic, AI and regulatory/security governance operate as one plane — and every decision and action is logged with full lineage back to FAA / EASA compliance requirements. The matrix maps the four classes of decision the factory must account for: human & machine, planned & emergent. Tap a cell for its governance analytics.

    Decision & action traceability matrix — tap a cell

    Phased AI learning autonomy is earned, not granted

    AI does not arrive autonomous. It climbs a gated ladder — each phase widens the circuit-breaker envelope only after the system proves itself on the five assurance dimensions (WHO · WHAT · WHEN · WHERE · WHY) and clears a reliability gate. Promotion is a governance decision, not a configuration flag — every AI model is also risk-tiered under the AI Center of Excellence before it earns its first phase.

    Digital twin · per site & across the enterprise

    Twins at the core of every site's planning

    Five twin levels — component, asset, process, facility and enterprise — federate across every site into one cross-enterprise picture, while each aircraft carries its own product twin from configuration through end of service life. Sites mature at their own pace; the newest campus leads, established sites follow.
